Elden Ring: Common Boss Strategies & Build Recommendations

📅 2026-09-08 · ✍️ Guides team · 🏷️ Boss Guide · 👁️ 8.3k

Your first journey through the Lands Between almost always stalls...

1. Universal combat rules

  • Observe first, strike later: read the wind-up before dodging.
  • Manage stamina: keep enough for at least two rolls.
  • Summons are a tool, not a crutch: they draw aggro.

2. Recommended starter builds

If this is your first Souls game, start with one of these:

BuildCore statsStrengths
Vagabond (Strength)Vigor / StrengthTanky, highest margin for error
Astrologer (Magic)Int / MindRanged damage, safe distance

3. Three signature fights

1. Margit, the Fell Omen

Your first 'Souls teacher'. His mix of fast and slow hits misleads timing:

  • Explore the area first — level up before him.
  • Summon the spirit wolf to draw aggro.
  • His daggers can be parried with a shield.

2. Godrick the Grafted

Phase one: stay behind his hind legs. Phase two: watch for the fire breath and run far.

3. Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

Break the three floating orbs in phase one; stay glued to her in phase two.

Tip: stuck? Go level up first. In Souls, 'stuck' often means under-leveled.

4. Closing words

The joy of Souls isn't 'never getting stuck' — it's the rush when you finally beat that boss. Good luck in the Lands Between.
