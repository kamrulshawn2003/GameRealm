Genshin Impact: Beginner Resource Allocation Guide

📅 2026-09-06 · ✍️ Guides team · 🏷️ Progression · 👁️ 12.6k

New Travelers in Teyvat are often overwhelmed by the progression systems...

1. Primogems: only for characters you truly love

Primogems are the scarcest resource — the top rule is don't pull every banner. Suggestions:

  • Secure one mature main DPS first.
  • Then build a support that enables elemental reactions.
  • Waifu/husbandos > meta. Long-term, love is what keeps you playing.

2. Mora & books: don't spread evenly

Many beginners spread materials across all four characters, leaving none strong. The right way:

PriorityInvest inNotes
Tier 1Main DPS + weaponMax level and talents first
Tier 2Core supportsJust enough for burst rotation
Tier 3Utility unitsEnough to cast skills and roam

3. Artifacts: don't chase perfection early

  • Before AR45, artifact fodder spent is wasted.
  • Wear purple artifacts with the right main stats; save resin for ascensions.
  • Farm artifact domains in earnest after AR45.

4. Daily must-dos

4 daily commissions → spend resin → claim reputation / BP rewards → log off

Keep this up daily and a month of accumulation surprises you.

5. Mindset

Genshin is a long game — there's no 'finish in a day'. Take it slow, follow the story, and the scenery of Teyvat is the reward itself.
