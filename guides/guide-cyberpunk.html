Cyberpunk 2077: Ending Triggers & Choice Branches

📅 2026-09-04 · ✍️ Guides team · 🏷️ Endings · 👁️ 9.7k

Night City doesn't give second chances — but the game does, offering V multiple exits. Endings depend on whom you befriended.

1. Ending overview

Main endings are decided in 'Nocturne Op55N1' — who you choose to trust. On Misty's rooftop:

ChoiceEnding lineKey requirement
Ask ArasakaThe DevilAvailable directly
Call PanamThe StarComplete Panam's arc
Call JudyThe SunComplete Judy's arc
Go aloneTemperance lineHigh Johnny affinity

2. Key side quests: the seeds of endings

To see all endings, complete these before the final mission:

  • Panam: complete her arc. Only then does she appear on the roof.
  • Judy: complete her arc. She'll join your final stand.
  • Rogue: complete 'Blistering Love' to unlock Johnny-led endings.

3. Johnny's affinity

A hidden value gates some endings — your bond with Johnny Silverhand:

  • Lending him your body and respecting his choices raises it.
  • The right replies at key nodes unlock the secret ending.
Spoiler: save before the rooftop — once you choose, there's no going back.

4. Our recommendation

First play: follow your gut. Then reload and explore the 'what if' branches. Night City's charm is that every choice carries weight.
