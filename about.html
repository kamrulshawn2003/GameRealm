About Game Realm

A group of players who love games — and enjoy talking about them seriously.

Who we are

'Game Realm' began as the final project of a web design course. We want to treat games as a culture and art form worth discussing seriously.

What we do

  • Game news: track releases, patches and industry trends.
  • Guides: help players skip the detours with clear, responsible writing.
  • Game research: scholarly discussion of narrative, mechanics and industry.

Extra features

  • Site-wide bilingual search (Ctrl+K) with live results.
  • Light / dark theme toggle, saved between visits.
  • Reading progress bar and 1–5 star article ratings.
  • Newsletter sign-up form with input validation.
  • Live channel filtering and animated stat counters.

Site structure

The site has 3 core channels and 12 content pages, meeting '3 channels, each with 3 sub-pages':

  • News: channel home + 3 detail pages (Wukong / Genshin / Elden Ring).
  • Guides: channel home + 3 guides (Elden Ring / Genshin / Cyberpunk).
  • Research: channel home + 3 essays (Narrative / Mechanics / Industry).
